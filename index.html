Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what information the OBD2 ELM327 Scanner Android app (package com.aemdev.carscanner) uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is developed by Ahmed Essam (AEM Dev), Egypt ("we", "us").

In short:

  • Your vehicles, VIN, trips, GPS data, sensor logs and diagnostic codes stay on your phone. We do not upload them.
  • No account or sign-in is needed.
  • Some data leaves your phone through Google services: crash reports, app analytics, ads, and issue reports you choose to send.
  • We do not sell your data.

1. Information stored only on your device

The following is saved in the app's private storage on your phone and is not sent to us:

  • Vehicle profiles you create (name, make, model, year, fuel type, optional VIN).
  • Trips, live sensor readings and logs, diagnostic trouble codes and their history.
  • Reports you export (PDF, CSV, JSON). These are only shared if you choose to share or save them.
  • Names and addresses of OBD-II adapters you connect to, and your app settings.
  • Location (GPS): used only while the app is open on screen, to show GPS speed, altitude and similar sensors and to add them to your trips. Location is saved with your trips on your phone and is not sent to us. The app does not use location in the background.

Ads and consent

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Some features (for example trip export and trip sensors) can be unlocked by watching an optional rewarded ad. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. On any Android device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device).

3. Permissions the app asks for

  • Bluetooth / Nearby devices: to find and connect to your OBD-II adapter.
  • Location: required by Android for Bluetooth scanning on some versions, and for GPS sensors while the app is open. Never used in the background.
  • Notifications: to show the ongoing notification while a trip is recording.
  • Internet: for Wi-Fi OBD adapters, ads, crash reports, analytics and issue reports.

You can deny any permission. The related feature will not work, but the rest of the app will.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We do not share it with anyone except the Google service providers listed above, who process it on our behalf under their own terms, or when required by law. Learn how Google uses data: How Google uses information from apps that use its services, Firebase privacy, and Google Privacy Policy.

5. How long data is kept

  • Data on your device: until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.
  • Issue reports: up to 12 months after they are received, then deleted.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Analytics and advertising data: kept according to Google's retention settings.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • On-device data: delete vehicles and trips in the app, or remove everything in Android Settings → Apps → OBD2 ELM327 Scanner → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • Issue reports: email us at ahmedessam291994@gmail.com with the subject "OBD2 ELM327 Scanner data deletion". Include the approximate date you sent the report and your device model so we can find it. We delete it within 30 days.
  • Ads: change your consent in Settings → Privacy & ad choices (where available) and manage your advertising ID in Android settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Contact us to use these rights.

7. Children

OBD2 ELM327 Scanner is a vehicle diagnostics tool meant for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above. Important changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
